Highlights

- First multimodal benchmark on PGUPharyngitis: images, 20 symptoms, age and sex.
- Phone model alone predicts expert labels (AUC 0.67), beating the published baseline.
- Image features identify the phone (AUC 0.99) but fail across phones (AUC ≈ 0.5).
- Within one phone, image AUC falls to 0.50–0.58; 18 colour statistics match deep nets.
- Symptom fusion adds nothing over images; benchmarks need site-aware evaluation.
