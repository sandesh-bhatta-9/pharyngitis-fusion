Highlights

- The phone model alone predicts expert pharyngitis labels with AUC 0.67.
- A deconfounded AUC scores phone-only models 0.5 and recovers the shortcut-free AUC.
- Deconfounded, image and fusion models fall from about 0.70 to 0.52–0.58.
- Concept erasure removes the phone signal but recovers no throat signal.
- Adversarial debiasing lowers AUC yet leaves the phone 97–100% identifiable.
